Greedy

What it is

Make the locally optimal choice at each step without backtracking. Proof is often exchange argument or "never worse to do X first."

When to use

  • Scheduling / ordering problems.
  • "Minimum number of …" with clear priority.
  • Interval covering, activity selection.
  • When sorting by a custom key makes the choice obvious.

Proving greedy (quick checklist)

  1. Greedy choice property: A global optimum exists that starts with your greedy choice.
  2. Optimal substructure: After greedy step, remainder is smaller instance of same problem.

If you can't prove it in 2 minutes during contest → try DP or binary search.
