Pigeonhole & Counting Arguments

What it is

Pigeonhole principle: If n items go into m boxes with n > m, some box has ≥ 2 items.

Generalized: if n items in m boxes, some box has ≥ ⌈n/m⌉ items.

When to use

  • Prove existence without construction.
  • Bound answer: "at least X" or "at most Y".
  • Duplicate in subarray of length > alphabet size.
  • Parity / coloring arguments.

Examples

1. Subarray with sum divisible by n

Prefix sums mod n: n+1 prefixes, n residues → two same → subarray sum ≡ 0.

2. Two numbers same mod k in range

Pigeonhole on residues.

3. CF 977C — Less or Equal

Counting argument for construction bounds.

4. Erdős-style: enough random picks → collision

Birthday paradox intuition.

5. Graph coloring

If max degree Δ, need at most Δ+1 colors — constructive pigeonhole in greedy.
