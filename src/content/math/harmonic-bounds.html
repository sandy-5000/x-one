Harmonic Bounds & Estimates

What it is

Harmonic number: H(n) = 1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + ... + 1/n ≈ ln(n) + γ.

In contests, use bounds not exact values:

H(n) = O(log n)
Σ_{i=1}^{n} 1/i ≈ ln(n) + 0.577...

When to use

  • Prove greedy is O(n log n).
  • Expected number of comparisons in quicksort-like analysis.
  • Count divisors: Σ_{i=1}^{n} τ(i) = Σ_{d=1}^{n} ⌊n/d⌋ ≈ n log n.
